# Example — Family with kids (one parent trains, kids just eat well)

Fictional, filled‑in example. Shows how one household mixes an athlete, a no‑goal adult, and children. **Made‑up numbers, not advice.**

## profiles.md (excerpt)
### Person 1 — Alex — cyclist, cutting
- M / 41 / 1.80 m · 80 kg · Body‑fat 22% → target 16% (hold 15–17%).
- Protein 2.0 g/kg. Carb bands: rest 3 / endurance 6 / hard 9 g/kg. Deficit ~350 kcal off resting; cap 0.4 kg/wk.
- Energy‑availability floor 30 kcal/kg FFM. Burn model ~1,800 + 9×TSS.

### Person 2 — Jordan — no cutting goal, feed well
- F / 39 · Maintain. Protein ~1.7 g/kg, moderate carbs. Prefers lighter dinners.

### Person 3 — Kid (7) & Person 4 — Kid (10)
- **No deficit, no targets.** Nutritious, age‑appropriate portions; variety; whole foods.
- Kid A: won't eat "mixed" foods — keep components separate. Kid B: loves anything with rice.

## restrictions-allergies.md (excerpt)
- Kid A: **tree‑nut allergy (HARD BLOCK — never include).**
- Jordan: dairy‑sensitive (soft — small amounts ok). Alex: none. Whole‑food bias for all.

## how-we-eat.md (excerpt)
- Family dinner together 6 nights; Friday is homemade pizza/taco night. 30‑min ceiling.
- Kid‑friendly anchors: taco bar, pasta (nut‑free), sheet‑pan chicken. Batch‑cook Sunday.
- Warehouse store for bulk + weekly grocery. Budget ~$220/wk for four.

## How the planner handles this household
- **One shared dinner, portioned per person:** kids get simple builds + their carb; Alex gets an extra rice/potato add‑on and more on hard‑ride days; Jordan gets a lighter plate.
- **Nut‑free is absolute** across every shared dish because of Kid A.
- Alex's macros periodize to his rides; the rest of the family just eats good, adequate food.
- Shopping list scales to 4 people and flags the bulk buys to amortize.

## A typical weekly note
- Alex has a hard interval day Tue and a long ride Sat → carb‑up Fri, zero deficit Sat, big post‑ride refuel. Kids and Jordan unaffected; same dinners, different portions.
