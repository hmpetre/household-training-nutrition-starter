# Profiles

Every tunable number the engine reads lives here. Add one block per person. Copy the block that fits — an **athlete with a goal**, a **general adult**, or a **child**. Delete what you don't use. Fill the `[ ]` blanks. If you don't know a number, leave it and let the planner use sensible defaults / your connectors.

> Not everyone needs macros or a goal. It's completely fine for a profile to say "no goal — just feed well."

### Macros
- **Protein: [1.6–2.2] g/kg bodyweight** — higher in a deficit to protect lean mass. Constant.
- **Carb bands (g/kg bodyweight), the DAILY TOTAL by training load:**
  - Rest day: [2–3]
  - Endurance / long easy: [5–8]
  - Hard or long (intervals, race, big TSS): [8–10+]
- **Fat:** fills the remainder to the calorie target; floor ~[0.6] g/kg for hormones — don't go below.

### Carb timing / periodization (WHEN, not just how much)
- Night before a key/hard session: shift carbs into that evening's (lower‑fat) meal.
- Pre‑workout (1–3 h before): ~1–2 g/kg, easy to digest.
- During (endurance sports): fuel longer/harder sessions; e.g. cycling can use ~[60–120] g carb/hr on hard/long rides — tell them how much to carry. Count on‑session carbs into the day's total.
- Post‑workout (0–2 h): ~1–1.2 g/kg carbs + [30–40] g protein.
- Rest days: keep carbs low (rest band); this is where any deficit sits.

### Cut / deficit rules (omit if goal is maintain/gain)
- Daily deficit target: **~[250–400] kcal**, taken off the RESTING side only.
- Weekly weight‑loss cap: **[0.45] kg/wk** (smaller in heavy blocks). If trend drops faster, shrink the deficit.
- Deficit placement: onto easy/rest days; hard/long days at maintenance+; key sessions/races at surplus carb‑up.
- Key session = no deficit. Define it: **any planned workout ≥ [120] TSS, or any event titled/known as a race.**

### Energy‑availability floor (absolute)
- Keep **(intake − exercise energy) ≥ [30] kcal per kg fat‑free mass.**
- Fat‑free mass ≈ weight × (1 − bodyfat). Compute the floor and never breach it — reduce the deficit, never the training fuel.

### Burn model — calibrate to this person over time
- **Whole‑day burn ≈ [baseline] kcal + [kcal_per_TSS] × TSS.** Starting defaults if you have nothing yet: baseline ≈ resting/daily‑life calories on a no‑training day (often ~1,500–1,900 for adults); kcal/TSS ≈ **9** as a reasonable central estimate for cyclists. Recalibrate monthly with real data (see `calibration-log.md`) and trust observed data over the formula.
